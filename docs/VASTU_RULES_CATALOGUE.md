# Vastu Shastra Rules Catalogue

## Purpose & Scope
This catalogue defines the architectural orientation rules used by the Vastu Shastra evaluation engine in the Intelligent House Design and Construction Assistant.

> **Important Notice**: Vastu recommendations in this platform are treated as traditional Indian architectural design guidelines for spatial harmony, lighting, and natural ventilation. They never supersede mandatory structural safety standards, national building codes (NBC), or municipal building bylaws.

---

## The 8 Cardinal and Inter-Cardinal Zones (Vastu Purusha Mandala)

| Direction / Zone | Sanskrit Name | Ruling Element | Recommended Room Placements | Unfavorable Placements |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **North-East (NE)** | *Ishanya* | Water / Mind | Pooja Room, Study Room, Open Verandah, Water Sump | Toilet, Kitchen, Master Bedroom, Heavy Stairs |
| **East (E)** | *Indra* | Solar / Light | Main Entrance, Living Room, Balcony | Heavy Storage, Toilets |
| **South-East (SE)**| *Agneya* | Fire | Primary Kitchen, Electrical Meter, Utility Area | Master Bedroom, Underground Water Tank, Pooja Room |
| **South (S)** | *Yama* | Earth | Bedrooms, Store Room, Staircase | Main Entrance (unless specific pada), Borewell |
| **South-West (SW)**| *Niruthi* | Earth / Stability| Master Bedroom, Wardrobes, Heavy Structure | Kitchen, Water Tank, Pooja Room, Main Entrance |
| **West (W)** | *Varuna* | Air / Water | Dining Room, Children's Bedroom, Study Room | Open Courtyard, Entrance facing negative pada |
| **North-West (NW)**| *Vayu* | Air / Motion | Guest Bedroom, Parking, Bathrooms, Pantry | Master Bedroom, Pooja Room |
| **North (N)** | *Kubera* | Wealth / Opportunity| Living Room, Cash/Locker Room, Open Lawn | Toilets, Septic Tank, Heavy Clutter |
| **Center** | *Brahmasthan*| Space / Ether | Open Courtyard / Circulation Hall | Heavy Pillars, Kitchen, Toilet, Staircase |

## Room-Wise Compliance Rules

### 1. Main Entrance (Simha Dwara)
- **High Compliance (100%)**: North-East, East (Jayanta, Indra padas), North (Mukhya, Bhallata padas).
- **Moderate (70%)**: West (Pushpadanta pada), South (Grihakshata pada).
- **Low (30%)**: South-West (*Niruthi*), South-East (*Agneya*).
- **Remedy / Suggestion**: If entrance is fixed in SW due to plot road access, provide an internal foyer redirection and subtle threshold separation.

### 2. Kitchen (*Agni Sthana*)
- **High Compliance (100%)**: South-East (*Agneya* zone) with stove facing East.
- **Moderate (75%)**: North-West (*Vayu* zone) as secondary cooking alternative.
- **Strictly Incompatible (0%)**: North-East (conflicts Water and Fire elements) or South-West.

### 3. Master Bedroom
- **High Compliance (100%)**: South-West (*Niruthi* zone) promoting stability, sleep quality, and load bearing.
- **Moderate (70%)**: South or West.
- **Incompatible (20%)**: North-East (leads to restlessness and light pollution) or South-East.

### 4. Pooja Room / Prayer Space
- **High Compliance (100%)**: North-East (*Ishanya* zone), East, or North.
- **Incompatible (0%)**: Adjacent to or directly below/above bathrooms; South-West.

### 5. Staircase
- **High Compliance (100%)**: South, South-West, or West turning clockwise (*Pradakshina*).
- **Incompatible (10%)**: Exact North-East corner or Center (*Brahmasthan*).

### 6. Bathrooms & Toilets
- **High Compliance (100%)**: North-West (*Vayu* zone) or West of South.
- **Incompatible (0%)**: North-East corner or Center (*Brahmasthan*).
